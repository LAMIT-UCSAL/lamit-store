## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This reads as a generic product-grid template with the LAMIT logo and Portuguese copy dropped in — not a page authored for LAMIT's scrapbook identity. Every element the brand guide calls out by name (torn/crumpled paper, masking tape, hand-drawn doodles, collage, white-outlined photo cutouts, textured backgrounds) is absent from the CSS; there's no texture, no clip-path, no SVG doodle. The typography token is an explicitly-flagged placeholder system font, so the "magazine/mural/notebook" typographic voice the brand demands doesn't exist yet. Product photography is clean seamless-grey studio shots, not the brand-mandated white-outline cutouts. ## Overall Impression

The page is functionally solid and mechanically clean — responsive grid, no console-breaking errors, decent information architecture for a 4-item catalog. But it is not yet a LAMIT page; it's a template that happens to have LAMIT's logo and product photos in it. The single biggest opportunity is the same insight in two forms: (1) implement the actual scrapbook/collage visual language the brand guide already fully specifies, and (2) stop asking people to click all the way to an external order form without ever telling them what it costs.

## What's Working

- **Delivery-note transparency** — the MOQ/crowd-order explanation in `product.deliveryNote` is the one place the copy actually sounds like "someone from inside the community talking to you," matching the brand's tone mandate even though the rest of the copy doesn't.
- **Restrained hover rotation** — the `-0.5°`/`-1°` transforms on `.card`/`.btn` hover are a small, correct, on-concept gesture toward "imperfect, handmade." Proof the team understands the brand, even though it isn't executed further.
- **Responsive grid discipline** — 1/2/4-column breakpoints with `aspect-ratio: 1/1` locked image wrappers give a clean, layout-shift-free foundation a fuller scrapbook treatment could be built on top of without re-architecting anything.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] No price shown anywhere in the funnel**
- **Why it matters:** Every product has `price: null`; neither the catalog cards nor the product detail page ever display a price or range before the "Pedir" CTA to an external Google Form. For a student audience this is the single highest-friction trust gap in a Persuade-mode flow — people either bounce before clicking through, or click through anxious and abandon at the form.
- **Fix:** At minimum, add "Preço em breve — você confirma antes de finalizar" near the CTA so the absence reads as intentional, not broken. Better: show an actual price/range as soon as suppliers confirm it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Zero implementation of the mandated scrapbook/collage visual language**
- **Why it matters:** This is the core creative concept of the brand ("grandes ideias começam como rascunhos") and it's entirely missing from the CSS — no texture, no tape, no doodles, placeholder typography. The page could carry any organization's logo and be indistinguishable.
- **Fix:** Paper texture on `.card`, a masking-tape corner accent, one hand-drawn SVG doodle near the intro, a real display font for headings.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder`

**[P1] Brand voice and core message missing from the homepage copy**
- **Why it matters:** The brand guide explicitly forbids sounding "corporativa, fria ou institucional," and the current intro paragraph is exactly that register. First-time visitors get no emotional hook, and the brand's own tagline concept never appears on the page.
- **Fix:** Rewrite the intro around the "rascunho"/community-insider framing, in first-person-plural community voice.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Ecobag card sells an empty product**
- **Why it matters:** The ecobag entry has no image, no sizes, no colors, and a "Descrição em breve" placeholder description, yet it renders identically to complete products and still links through to a live "Pedir" CTA. Users can attempt to order something they've seen zero real information about.
- **Fix:** Either suppress/disable the CTA and mark the card "Em breve" until content exists, or pull it from the grid until ready.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] No Open Graph/meta tags for social sharing**
- **Why it matters:** This drop is meant to spread through Instagram/WhatsApp links inside an academic community — the first impression (the link preview card) will currently show a bare, unbranded blue link, losing the "peak" moment before the visitor even lands.
- **Fix:** Add title/description/`og:image` (ideally a scrapbook-styled share image) and a favicon.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

## Minor Observations

- Cards show name + category but no visible "Ver produto →" affordance — the whole-card link works functionally but offers no visual invitation to click.
- The brand's accent color `#E8722C` never appears on the homepage itself — only inside `.btn` on the PDP — so the catalog page never uses its own accent color at all.
- `aria-label="Ver ${product.name}"` on the card overrides the accessible name entirely, making the visible `<h3>` name somewhat redundant for screen reader users (not broken, just duplicated effort).
- Color contrast checks passed comfortably (`--color-primary` on `--color-bg` ~5:1; navy-on-orange `.btn` combo) — no accessibility contrast issue found.
- Delivery-note copy is identical boilerplate across all 4 products — fine for MVP, a missed chance to differentiate voice per item later.
- Assessment B's environment note: the Bash tool was blocked by a permission hook when invoking the detector and had to fall back to PowerShell (succeeded); this is operational friction worth knowing about for future automated `/impeccable` runs in this environment, not a design finding.
